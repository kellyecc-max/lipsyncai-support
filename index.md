# LipSyncAI Support

Need help or have a question? Email **[lipsyncaisupport@gmail.com](mailto:lipsyncaisupport@gmail.com)** and include your iPhone model and iOS version. I usually reply within two business days.

## Requirements

- iPhone 13 or newer
- Camera and microphone access

## Common questions

**The camera won't start.**
Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera and turn on LipSyncAI. Do the same under Microphone. Then return to the app.

**I got an "Ambiguous Result."**
The app found two possible offsets that were close in score. Measure again with the speaker's face clearly visible and continuous speech, or use Multi Sample mode.

**My result says Low confidence.**
Improve the lighting, fill more of the frame with the speaker's face, and reduce background noise. Multi Sample mode gives the most reliable results.

**Do I need to calibrate?**
No. Calibration is optional and removes your iPhone's own small camera and microphone delay, typically under 15 ms. It is found under Settings > Advanced.

**How accurate is it?**
Results typically agree within about 10 ms. Before making critical adjustments to a live or broadcast system, confirm with a second measurement.

**What do Audio Leads and Audio Lags mean?**
Audio Leads Video means the sound arrives before the lips move, so add audio delay. Audio Lags Video means the sound arrives late, so delay the video or move the audio earlier. Tap any result for specific correction guidance.

## Privacy

LipSyncAI processes everything on your device. No audio or video is recorded, stored, or sent anywhere. Read the full [Privacy Policy](https://kellyecc-max.github.io/lipsyncai-privacy/).
